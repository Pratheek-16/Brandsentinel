# Task 4: Ambiguity Detection & KB Clarification (I-3)

## Participant Role: Brand Guardian / Administrator (`admin@novafit.local`)
**Goal:** Detect ambiguous guidelines with high creator dismissals and update the active Knowledge Base version in one click.

### Instructions
1. Log in with admin credentials.
2. Navigate to the **"Insights & Ambiguity (I-3)"** tab.
3. Review the 4 Governance Signal cards:
   - Signal 1: High Creator Dismissals (>40%)
   - Signal 2: Conflicting Rules & Facts in Active KB
   - Signal 3: Stale / Expired Facts
   - Signal 4: AI vs Human Disagreements
4. Locate rule `NF-TONE-01` or another flagged rule with high human dismissal rates.
5. Review the proposed clarified wording generated by the engine.
6. Click **"Accept Clarification"**.
7. Confirm that the card transitions to *"Resolved in vNext"* and that a new active KB version is activated.
8. Record completion timestamp.
